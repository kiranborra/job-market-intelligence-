KEY FINDINGS  (paste these into the README)
========================================================================
Dataset: 1,569 live postings from 774 companies, posted 2026-05-24 to 2026-08-23.

1. Entry-level roles are scarce. Only 48 of 1,569 postings (3.1%) are junior, graduate or internship level. The rest ask for mid-level experience or above.

2. Hiring is concentrated in a few cities. The top three account for 57.8% of postings that name a city, with Bangalore alone at 31.3%.

3. A single employer dominates. Accenture posted 169 roles (10.8% of the market).

4. Each role family has a signature tool:
     Data Engineer            ETL (61.3% of its postings)
     Business Intelligence    Power BI (41.3% of its postings)
     Data Scientist           Machine Learning (30.9% of its postings)
     Business Analyst         Stakeholder Management (26.0% of its postings)
     Data Analyst             SQL (17.5% of its postings)

   So what: the five job titles are not interchangeable. Applying to 'data roles' generically wastes effort - the tool you learn should follow the role you target.

Method note: skills were detected in 56% of postings. The Adzuna API truncates descriptions to ~500 characters, so absolute percentages understate demand; comparisons between roles are the reliable signal.
